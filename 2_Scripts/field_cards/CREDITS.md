- Short-tailed bandicoot rat (*Nesokia indica*)
- Steppe field mouse (*Sylvaemus witherbyi*)
- Tian Shan birch mouse (*Sicista tianshanica*)
- Tundra shrew (*Sorex tundrensis*)
