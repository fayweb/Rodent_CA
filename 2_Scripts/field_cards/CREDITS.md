# Photograph credits

Every photograph in the Merke field cards, with photographer, licence and source.
Sourced from iNaturalist filtered to CC0 and CC-BY only; iNaturalist's default photo
licence is CC BY-NC, which is not used here. Selections prefer observations annotated
as a whole organism, so tracks, scat and bone records are excluded.

Regenerate with `python fetch_photos.py`, then `python render_cards.py`.
Review or swap with `python review_photos.py` and `--pin "Binomial=obs_id"`.

## Species with no openly licensed photograph

images that exist, none is openly licensed. They are the trip shot list.

- Dwarf fat-tailed jerboa (*Pygeretmus pumilio*)
- Short-tailed bandicoot rat (*Nesokia indica*)
- Steppe field mouse (*Sylvaemus witherbyi*)
- Tian Shan birch mouse (*Sicista tianshanica*)
- Tundra shrew (*Sorex tundrensis*)
